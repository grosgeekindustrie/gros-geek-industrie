Version : `1.0-candidate` · locale cible : `en`.

> Validé sur le lot test de quatre fiches et quarante localisations, note globale 8,5/10.

## Note de marché

Use natural international/US Etsy hobby English. 'Unpainted' is more idiomatic and searchable than a literal 'figure to paint'.

## Formulations préférées

- `sculptor_label` : **Sculptor:**

- `collectible_figure` : **collectible figure** · **collectible resin figure**
- `collectible_statue` : **collectible statue** · **resin statue**
- `paintable` : **unpainted figure** · **figure for painters**
- `ready_to_paint` : **ready to prime and paint** · **prepared and ready to paint**
- `garage_kit` : **resin garage kit** · **unpainted resin garage kit**
- `resin_kit` : **resin model kit** · **resin figure kit**
- `3d_printed` : **3D-printed resin figure** · **3D-printed resin statue**
- `painter_gift` : **gift for a figure painter** · **gift for a miniature painter**
- `geek_gift` : **anime fan gift** · **gamer gift** · **gift for fans**
- `collector_gift` : **gift for a figure collector** · **collector gift**

## Formulations à éviter

- figure to paint
- painter gift
- 3D printer figure
- garage figure
- statue to paint
